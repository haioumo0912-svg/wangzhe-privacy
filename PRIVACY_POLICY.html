中文

王者短剧（包名 com.sdno3.app，以下简称“本应用”）提供短剧浏览、搜索、播放、收藏及播放记录功能。本政策适用于本应用的测试版本和正式版本。

1. 我们处理的信息

使用浏览、搜索和播放功能时，本应用会向内容服务发送完成相应请求所需的信息。内容服务可能接收 IP 地址、请求时间、请求内容和设备网络信息。

播放记录、收藏、视频缓存以及无法通过邮件应用发送的反馈内容保存在设备本地。您可以通过清除应用数据或卸载应用删除这些本地内容。使用“用户反馈”时，您可以自行选择邮件应用发送所填写的内容；发送后的处理也受您选择的邮件服务政策约束。

2. 广告与隐私选择

本应用集成 Google AdMob 和 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）SDK，并可能展示插屏广告。内部测试和封闭测试版本使用 Google 官方测试广告；正式版本可使用正式广告 ID。无论使用测试广告还是真实广告，相关 SDK 都可能按其政策处理广告标识符、IP 地址、设备信息、广告互动和诊断信息，以提供广告、衡量效果、防范欺诈并履行法律义务。我们不会因为切换广告 ID 而改变本政策所述的数据类别或处理目的。

在适用地区，本应用通过 UMP 获取和管理广告隐私选择，并仅在 SDK 判断可以请求广告时发起广告请求。需要提供隐私选项时，您可以在“我的”页面打开“广告隐私选项”管理选择。表单是否出现取决于所在地区、同意状态及 Google 后台配置。

Google 隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google 广告数据说明：https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. 信息共享与保存

为提供内容和广告，本应用可能与内容服务及上述 Google 服务共享必要信息。设备本地数据保留至您清除应用数据或卸载应用；第三方对其收到信息的保存与处理适用各自的隐私政策。我们不出售您的个人信息。

English

王者短剧 (package name: com.sdno3.app, “the App”) offers short drama browsing, search, playback, favorites, and watch history. This policy applies to both testing and production versions of the App.

1. Information we process

When you browse, search, or play content, the App sends information needed to fulfill your request to its content service. The service may receive your IP address, request time, requested content, and network information.

Watch history, favorites, video cache, and feedback that cannot be sent through an email app are stored on your device. You can remove this local data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. If you use User Feedback, you choose whether to send your message through an email app; your chosen email provider's policy also applies after sending.

2. Advertising and privacy choices

The App integrates the Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs and may display interstitial ads. Internal and closed testing versions use Google's official test ads; production versions may use live ad IDs. In either case, these SDKs may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information under their policies to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Switching ad IDs does not change the categories of data or purposes described here.

Where applicable, the App uses UMP to collect and manage advertising privacy choices and requests ads only when the SDK indicates that it may do so. When required, you can manage your choices through Advertising Privacy Options on the My page. Whether a form appears depends on your region, consent status, and Google's configuration.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The App may share information necessary to provide content and advertising with its content service and the Google services described above. The App is not specifically directed to children. If the App's features, third-party services, or data practices materially change, we will update and publish this policy. For questions or requests concerning this policy or your personal information, email haioumo0912@gmail.com.
